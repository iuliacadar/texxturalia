# Brand — Texxturalia

> The aesthetic constitution of the project. Everything visual and conceptual
> flows from this document. (Romanian nuances are kept alongside the English
> terms where the wordplay matters.)

## The Four Pillars

| Pillar | Source | Principle |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundation** | Constantin Brâncuși | Reduction to essence. Pure form, freed of decorative noise. *"Simplicity is not a goal in art, but one arrives at it in spite of oneself, as we approach the real meaning of things."* |
| **Atmosphere** | Richard Moult | Mystic naturalism. An aesthetic of solitude, telluric landscape, heavy near-religious stillness. |
| **Rhythm** | Ikebana | Asymmetric tension. The void (white space) is not empty — it is an active element of composition. |
| **Super-atmosphere** | Elsa Schiaparelli | The object as extension of the body. A tactile deconstruction turning garment and accessory into visceral symbols. |

## Palette: Bone & Oxide ("Os & Oxid")

Industrial colors are replaced with organic tones that suggest the passage of
time and the transformation of matter.

| Name | Hex | Role | Symbolism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bone / Os | `#f2f0eb` | Universal background | Structure; what remains; foundation |
| Oxide / Oxid | `#dcd7cf`, `#333` | Image containers, logo | Matter reacting with time |
| Text / Oxid dark | `#2c2c2c` | Global text & line elements | Organic black — never industrial black |
| Oxidized silver | `#c5c5c5` | Structural actives (menu bars, main dividers) | Balance between ethereal and functional |

The low contrast between bone background and oxide text removes digital
aggressiveness — the site reads like a physical, timeless archive.

## The Conceptual Triad

The brand's central sentence:

> **(De(Con))Struction of the thread. Betwixt and between the atavic raiment,
> the telluric plane, and the sacrality of the sidereal wire.**
> RO: *(De(Con))Strucția firului între veșmântul atavic, planul teluric și
> sacralitatea firului sideral.*

- **Atavic raiment** (*veșmântul atavic*) → the textiles. The body's first
  enclosure; textile does not clothe, it shelters.
- **Telluric plane** (*planul teluric*) → the decor objects. Chosen over
  "telluric space" for its philosophical rigor (a level of reality, a plane of
  immanence). Objects seem pulled straight from the soil.
- **Sidereal wire** (*firul sideral*) → the jewelry. Copper and silver as
  "sidereal" materials: copper = tamed stellar fire (Venus, first metal shaped
  by man); silver = cooled sidereal light (the Moon, mirror-metal of memory).
  Wire-wrapping as *pathei-mathos*: matter "suffers" through hands into form.

Hero header: **"Questioning the Abyss"** / RO *"Interogând Abisul"*.

## Concept Glossary

- **Atavic** — traits/instincts reappearing from a distant, primitive past;
  wild elemental drives lying in the subconscious.
- **Primordial** — existing from the very beginning; the originar element.
- **Pathei mathos** (πάθει μάθος) — "learning through suffering", from
  Aeschylus' *Agamemnon*; wisdom gained through lived experience.
- **Sidereal** — of the stars (lat. *sideris*); cosmic, atemporal, cold vast
  distance. Strictly, meteoric iron is the only true "sidereal metal";
  copper/silver earn it by planetary correspondence and stellar origin.
- **Betwixt and between** — liminality (Victor Turner): the transitional state
  where the thread stops being metal or fiber and becomes sacred object.
- **The Chasm** — replaced "The Void" (commercially worn-out); suggests a
  geological rupture, telluric and visceral.

## The Mythological Bestiary

Functional categories were replaced by tutelary figures — each showcase is a
ritual of transformation of matter:

| Figure | Domain | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Arachne** | Atavic Envelopes (textiles) | Instinct and the primordial web; textile woven as an extension of the body |
| **Corvus** | Sacral Scripts (jewelry) | The raven's script; writing in metal — copper/silver as calligraphy in space |
| **Leto / Lykeios** | Telluric Dialectics (decor) | The wolf; telluric force marking & protecting domestic territory |
| **Actaeon** | InterTexxturalia (guests) | The intruding curious gaze; invited designers looking into the universe |
| **Hermes-Thoth // Gnosis** | Manifesto | Creation as mediation between spirit and matter; knowing through touch |

## Voice & Naming Decisions

- **"Texxt Us"** / RO **"Texxtualizează"** — contact renamed to anchor writing
  into brand identity. No forms: a direct `mailto:` forces conscious, manual
  interaction, like touching the thread.
- **English-first**: root files are 100% English; Romanian lives exclusively
  in `/ro/` — for user clarity and search-engine hygiene.
- Typography as sculpture: Cormorant Garamond Italic for titles (poetic,
  human), Montserrat for body (modern stability). Content centered at 700px —
  publisher/gallery line length.

## Photographic Direction

Organic focus on texture, natural melancholic light (see Instagram:
[@t_e_x_x_t_u_r_a_l_i_a](https://www.instagram.com/t_e_x_x_t_u_r_a_l_i_a/)).
Keep asymmetry and breathing room; macro detail shots beside full views;
earthy tones extending naturally into the Bone/Oxide palette. Color is the
material truth of the pieces (copper, turquoise, amber) — B&W is a rescue
tool, never the default.
