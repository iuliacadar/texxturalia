The Clock of Waters

Orologiul apelor

Twelve telluric seas, gathered at the throat like the hours of a clock that measures not time — but longing. Each turquoise cabochon is a sea locked in its own copper net: water held by wire, the earth that held the water, a bath of amber light in an eternal August.

A necklace woven by hand, knot after knot. Twelve nets, twelve silences, a single gesture that will never be repeated. The chain that binds the seas is handmade too — each link a breath, each closure a seal of the atelier.

Material: Natural turquoise, copper
Technique: Wire wrapping, hand-made chain
Closure: Handmade
Length: Princess, approx. 50 cm — rests at the base of the throat
Availability: Unique piece
